# XPi on the Stellar Network

**Overcoming Bitcoin’s Limitations and Moving Beyond Pi Network’s Weaknesses**

### Bitcoin: Digital Gold Principles and Real-World Barriers

Bitcoin uses Proof-of-Work with a fixed supply of 21 million. Private keys ensure true ownership. However, the mining mechanism creates large barriers: expensive ASIC hardware and massive electricity consumption. Ordinary people can barely participate in the initial distribution.

### Pi Network from a Technical Perspective

In its early stages, “mining” Pi was mainly an app check-in. Users were not given real private keys; balances were stored on centralized servers. Closed source code, multi-year mainnet delays, and a very large total supply created significant inflationary pressure. These are fundamental differences from a complete on-chain blockchain.

### Advantages of Stellar that XPi Leverages

- Stellar Consensus Protocol (no energy-intensive mining)
- Built-in DEX
- Path payments
- Memos
- Trustlines (safety mechanism)
- Soroban smart contracts
- Extremely low fees and finality in a few seconds
- Ability to run nodes on ordinary hardware

### How XPi Combines the Strengths

XPi preserves Bitcoin’s scarcity principles (fixed supply + extremely slow distribution) while removing hardware barriers by allowing XLM staking from a very low minimum. Everything runs on-chain from day one, and users hold their own private keys.

The result is an asset that is scarce enough to aim for long-term store-of-value function, yet accessible enough for ordinary people to own.
